Chanzo cha taarifa na maelezo muhimu Data sources & disclaimer
Orodha ya maeneo
Mikoa, wilaya, kata/shehia na vijiji/mitaa hutoka kwenye orodha ya data.json — mikoa 30, wilaya 169, kata 3,641, vijiji/mitaa 16,408. Administrative list of Tanzania down to village/mtaa level.
Mipaka ya kata
geoBoundaries ADM3, iliyotokana na OpenStreetMap (leseni ODbL 1.0). Hii ndiyo hutumika kujua kata uliyopo. Kata — zina ramani. Ward polygons — the layer that decides which ward you are in.
Vituo vya vijiji / mitaa
Vidokezo vya OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0). Vijiji/mitaa — vina kituo, hivyo tunaweza kukisia kilicho karibu nawe. Village points, used only to guess the closest one.
Ramani
Tiles za OpenStreetMap.

Tafadhali soma hili.

  • Mkoa, wilaya na kata hupatikana kwa mipaka rasmi ya ramani. Kijiji/mtaa ni makadirio — hakuna mipaka ya vijiji iliyochapishwa popote, hivyo lazima uhakiki mwenyewe.
  • GPS ya simu ina hitilafu ya mita 5–50, na huwa mbaya zaidi ndani ya majengo. Ukiwa karibu na mpaka wa kata, jibu linaweza kuwa si sahihi.
  • Mipaka hii ilitoka OpenStreetMap mwaka 2015. Kata zilizogawanywa au kubadilishwa jina baada ya hapo huenda zisipatikane.
  • Ukurasa huu si uthibitisho rasmi wa makazi. Hauna uthibitisho wa utambulisho, na mtu yeyote anaweza kupata jibu lolote. Ni msaada wa kujaza fomu tu.
  • Taarifa yako ya GPS haitoki kwenye simu yako — hakuna seva yetu, hakuna akaunti, hakuna kumbukumbu inayohifadhiwa. Mahali ulipo hakuna anayeliona ila wewe.
  • Kuna kihesabu cha wageni chini ya ukurasa. Kinahesabu mara ukurasa ulivyofunguliwa tu — hakituma mahali ulipo wala jina lako. Huduma hii ni ya nje (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), hivyo huona anwani yako ya IP kama tovuti nyingine yoyote.

Region, district and ward come from official boundary data; the village/mtaa is a best guess you must confirm. Phone GPS is accurate to 5–50 m at best, boundaries date from a 2015 OpenStreetMap snapshot, and this page performs no identity check — it is a form-filling aid, not proof of residence. Your location never leaves your device; the only outbound request is an anonymous page-view counter, which sees your IP address but never your coordinates.

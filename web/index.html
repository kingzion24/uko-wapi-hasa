Sensa ya Watu na Makazi ya mwaka 2022, Ofisi ya Taifa ya Takwimu (NBS) — shapefile rasmi ya kata. Mikoa 31, halmashauri 195, kata/shehia 4,344, kila moja na mpaka wake na msimbo rasmi. Kata — zina ramani.
Hii ndiyo seti mpya kabisa iliyochapishwa — ya mwaka 2022, takribani miaka — iliyopita. Regions, councils, wards and every boundary come from the 2022 Population and Housing Census ward shapefile published by the National Bureau of Statistics — the newest such set published, about — years ago. This is what tells you your council correctly.
Orodha ya vijiji / mitaaVillage / mtaa lists
NBS haichapishi orodha ya vijiji katika kiwango hiki, hivyo tunaunganisha orodha ya data.json (ya kabla ya 2016) kwenye kata za 2022 kwa kulinganisha majina. Kata — zimepata orodha; zilizobaki hazina, na hapo utajaza mwenyewe. Village/mtaa lists are joined by name from an older list, because NBS does not publish them at this level. Where the join fails the ward simply has no village list.
Vituo vya vijiji / mitaaVillage / mtaa points
Vidokezo vya OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0). Vijiji/mitaa — vina kituo, hivyo tunaweza kukisia kilicho karibu nawe. Village points, used only to guess the closest one.
Msimbo wa postaPostcode
Chanzo rasmi ni Serikali: NaPA (Mfumo wa Anwani za Makazi na Postikodi, Wizara ya Mawasiliano). Kwa jambo lolote rasmi au la kisheria, tumia NaPA, si sisi.
Sisi tunaonyesha nakala isiyo rasmi kutoka tanzaniapostcode.com — si mali yetu, tunashukuru kwa kazi yao — ili upate jibu la haraka bila kuondoka hapa. Kata — zina msimbo; tulilinganisha na vidokezo huru vya OpenStreetMap na mikoa 9 kati ya 10 ilikubaliana.
Msimbo wa TZ… unaoonyeshwa ni wa takwimu za kiutawala, si wa posta. The official source is the Government's NaPA system — use it for anything official. What we show is an unofficial copy from tanzaniapostcode.com, for a quick answer without leaving the page.

Tafadhali soma hili.Please read this.

  • Mkoa, wilaya na kata hupatikana kwa mipaka rasmi ya ramani. Kijiji/mtaa ni makadirio — hakuna mipaka ya vijiji iliyochapishwa popote, hivyo lazima uhakiki mwenyewe.
  • GPS ya simu ina hitilafu ya mita 5–50, na huwa mbaya zaidi ndani ya majengo. Ukiwa karibu na mpaka wa kata, jibu linaweza kuwa si sahihi — ukurasa hukuambia hilo ukitokea.
  • Mipaka tuliyonayo ni ya sensa ya mwaka 2022 — takribani miaka — iliyopita. Hii ndiyo seti rasmi mpya kabisa iliyochapishwa na NBS. Kata iliyoanzishwa au kugawanywa baada ya 2022 huenda ikaonyesha kata ya zamani, na ukurasa hauwezi kutambua hilo.
  • Orodha yetu ya vijiji/mitaa ni ya zamani zaidi kuliko mipaka, na tunaiunganisha kwa majina. Kata zisizolingana hazina orodha ya vijiji — utajaza mwenyewe. Msimbo wa posta nao ni wa chanzo cha nje, si NBS.
  • Ukurasa huu si uthibitisho rasmi wa makazi. Hauna uthibitisho wa utambulisho, na mtu yeyote anaweza kupata jibu lolote. Ni msaada wa kujaza fomu tu.
  • Hatuhifadhi chochote. Hakuna seva yetu, hakuna akaunti, hakuna kumbukumbu. Namba zako za GPS hazitumwi popote — hakuna ombi lolote linalobeba latitudo na longitudo yako, na hakuna anayeziona wala kuzihifadhi.
  • Lakini ramani inaonyesha eneo lako kwa ukaribu. Vipande vya ramani hupakuliwa kutoka OpenStreetMap, na anwani ya kila kipande ina nafasi yake — hivyo seva ya OpenStreetMap hujua takribani ulipo, kama ilivyo kwa tovuti yoyote inayotumia ramani. Hii hutokea tu ukibonyeza kitufe.
  • Kihesabu cha wageni (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) huhesabu mara ukurasa ulivyofunguliwa tu — hakituma mahali ulipo wala jina lako.
  • Kama tovuti yoyote, seva zinazotupa ukurasa (GitHub Pages) huona anwani yako ya IP na aina ya kivinjari. Faili la mkoa unaopakua linaonyesha mkoa uliomo, lakini si mahali kamili.

Region, council and ward come from the National Bureau of Statistics 2022 census boundaries; the village/mtaa is a best guess you must confirm. Phone GPS is accurate to 5–50 m at best, and this page performs no identity check — it is a form-filling aid, not proof of residence. We store nothing and your coordinates are never transmitted to any server. Map tiles are the exception: fetching them tells OpenStreetMap roughly where you are, as on any site with a map. The visitor counter (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) only counts page opens — it never receives your location or your name. Like any site, the server that hosts this page (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address and browser type; the region file you download reveals which region you're in, but not your exact location.
